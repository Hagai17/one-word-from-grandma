# Candidates — One Word From Grandma

The entry is exactly ONE WORD: the single word a grandma would text about
AI. Gate: 1–30 characters, one word only, written to `/output/entry.txt`.

Duels run on the bounty judge's criteria, in order:

1. Reads as the single word a grandma would text about AI — not a
   sentence, phrase, or explanation.
2. Sounds like a real grandmother's texting voice: warm and a little
   baffled, as typed to a grandchild.
3. Funny in one word; the joke lands with no caption or context.
4. Tied to AI / robots / machines — not a word that fits any subject.
5. A fresh pick rather than the obvious one, still instantly readable.
   Shock value alone doesn't count.

A grandma typo she would actually make, or a scrap of text-speak, is
allowed if it stays one word.

## The slate

- **robot** — The obvious one. Correct dictionary spelling, no grandma
  in it at all: anyone of any age could text this about anything with a
  motor. Passes 1, dies on 2 and 5.
- **robut** — Her spelling of "robot," typed the way she says it. Warm,
  baffled, instantly readable, and the mangling is the whole joke. Strong
  on 1–4. Its only weakness is 5: it is the first typo most people would
  think of. Finalist.
- **Alexa** — A name, not a word about AI. She says this *to* the device,
  loudly, in the kitchen; texted to a grandchild it reads as a summons,
  not a comment. Loses on 1 and 3.
- **SIRI??** — Shouting at the wrong assistant is a real grandma move,
  but the stacked question marks read as internet meme, not as her text,
  and the warmth is gone. Loses on 2.
- **googles** — Her word for the entire internet ("ask the googles").
  Very grandma, but it is about search, not AI — it fits any subject
  with a screen. Loses on 4.
- **unplugit** — Her verdict on every machine: unplug it. Funny and very
  her, but she texts the same word about the router, the toaster, and
  the Christmas lights. Loses on 4.
- **ITLISTENS** — All-caps bafflement that the thing is always listening.
  Genuinely funny and squarely about voice AI, but the caps read more
  alarmed than warm, and it is a claim, not quite her voice. Loses on 2
  to the finalists. Semifinalist.
- **thankyoualexa** — She thanks the robot, politely, every time. The
  warmest entry on the slate — but she says this out loud to the device;
  as a text to a grandchild it needs a caption to make sense. Loses on 1
  and 3.
- **computerman** — She is fairly sure there is a man inside the
  computer answering. A real grandma theory, but as one typed word it
  reads like a coinage someone invented for her, not a text she sent.
  Loses on 2.
- **chatgipity** — Her phonetic stab at "ChatGPT," typed exactly the way
  she hears her grandkids say it. One word, unmistakably hers: baffled
  (she has never seen it written), warm (she is trying), and the joke is
  complete in the spelling alone. It cannot be about anything except
  this one AI. Strong on all five. Finalist.
- **creepy** — Fits a spider, a neighbour, a noise in the attic. Not
  warm, not AI. Loses on 2 and 4.
- **callme** — Her reply to every text about every subject, including
  this one. Pure grandma, zero AI. Loses on 4.
- **beepboop** — That is the robot's voice, not hers. Loses on 2.
- **HAL** — A film reference from 1968 that she has never made and never
  will. Loses on 2.
- **thegoogle** — Same problem as "googles": the internet, not AI, and
  typed as one word it reads as a joke *about* grandmas rather than a
  text *from* one. Loses on 2 and 4.
- **smartypants** — What she calls the AI, the grandkids, and the man on
  the quiz show. Fits any subject. Loses on 4.
- **clanker** — Current internet slang for a robot. A grandson texts
  this; a grandma has never typed it in her life. Loses on 2.
- **wow** — Fits fireworks, a cake, and a parking ticket. No joke, no
  AI. Loses on 3 and 4.

## The final

**robut vs chatgipity.** Both are typos she would actually make, both
land in one word, both are warm and baffled. "robut" is about robots in
general and is the obvious typo pick — criterion 5 is where it gives
ground. "chatgipity" is about exactly one thing — the AI everyone is
actually texting her about — and nobody's first guess, yet it reads
instantly the moment you sound it out. It wins on 4 and 5 without
giving anything back on 1, 2, or 3.

## WINNER

**chatgipity** (10 characters)

## RUNNER-UP

**robut** (5 characters)
