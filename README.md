A tiny Python program that writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`: the single word a grandma would text about AI — her phonetic stab at the chatbot's name, typed exactly the way she hears her grandkids say it, warm and a little baffled, with the whole joke carried by the spelling. The word was picked by drafting eighteen candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — reads as the one word she would text first, then her real texting voice, whether the joke lands in a single word, how squarely it is tied to AI, and freshness over the obvious pick. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
